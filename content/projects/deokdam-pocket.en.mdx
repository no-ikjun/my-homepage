## Why

In Korea the new year comes with *deokdam*, short wishes exchanged between people who care about each other. DeokDam Pocket gives that ritual a place online: a group, a pocket of messages, and a moment to look back before looking forward.

## What I built

- Groups for sharing greetings with a chosen circle.
- Writing, collecting and revisiting greetings.
- A year-in-review flow and New Year's resolutions.
- "Talk to future me", an AI conversation seeded from your own reflections.

Built and shipped solo with Next.js and PostgreSQL, launched for the 2024 new year and reopened each new year for two seasons. The service closed in January 2026.
