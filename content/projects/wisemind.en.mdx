## Why

Financial data is abundant but reading it takes expertise. Wisemind lets you ask questions in plain language and get an analysis with the numbers behind it.

## What I built

- A three-service architecture: a React frontend, a NestJS API, and a FastAPI AI server that talks to ChatGPT and HyperCLOVA X.
- Prompt and tool design so the model works from actual financial data rather than general knowledge.
- Deployment on AWS behind Cloudflare.

## What I learned

Building a product on top of two different LLM providers taught me a lot about abstraction boundaries, evaluation, and where model output needs guardrails before it reaches a user.
