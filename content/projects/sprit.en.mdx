## Why

Most people want to read more and stop after a week. SPRIT is built around the small mechanics that keep a habit alive: tracking, a goal you can actually hit, a nudge at the right time, and other people seeing you show up.

## What I built

- **Reading log and stats.** Sessions, pages and time, summarised so progress is visible.
- **Goals and achievements.** Short-horizon targets with a reward loop.
- **Reminders.** Reading-time notifications tuned to the user's own schedule.
- **Social sharing.** Post a reading status to keep yourself accountable.

I planned the product, designed the UI in Figma, built the Flutter app and the NestJS backend, and run it on AWS with Docker.

## Outcome

Live on the app stores since 2024 and still maintained.
