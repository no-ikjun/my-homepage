## Why

Campus notices were posted across a dozen channels, so students missed things that mattered. Ziggle collects them in one place, lets organisations post directly, and tells students when something new lands.

## What I built

- Took part in the initial planning and the shape of the product.
- Built the NestJS backend and PostgreSQL schema behind notice creation, categories and search.
- Designed the UI used by both the web and Flutter clients.

## Outcome

Ziggle became the standard way to publish notices at GIST and is still run by InfoTeam.
